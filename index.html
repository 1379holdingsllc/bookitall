Questions? Quick answers.

What happens after I send a request?

A real person reviews it and follows up by phone or text, usually the same day, to ask any questions and confirm the details and price.

Is my request a confirmed booking?

No. Sending a request is free and doesn't book or charge anything. Nothing is booked until we confirm the details and price with you and you say yes.

What details should I include?

What needs doing, roughly how big the job is, any supplies already on site, parking or stairs, and your timing. Photos help too: text them to us after you send the form.

Do I need to provide supplies or materials?

Tell us what you already have. If the provider needs to bring supplies or parts, we'll tell you before you confirm whether they're included in the price.

Do you take commercial jobs?

Yes. Switch to “Commercial” at the top of the page for office cleaning, janitorial, pressure washing, property maintenance, retail and restaurant cleaning, and HOA or property manager work. One-time and recurring jobs are both welcome.

Does it cost anything to post a job or send a bid?

No. Posting a job is free, and pros can browse open jobs and send bids for free. You only pay once you pick a pro and confirm the job.

I'm a pro. How do I get jobs?

Join free on the “Join as a pro” page with your services, ZIP codes and availability, then browse open jobs and send a bid on any you want. We text you when new jobs in your area are posted.

How do I pay?

There's no charge to send a request. Once you confirm a booking, you pay by PayPal invoice. Providers are paid after the job or rental is completed.

Can I change or cancel my request?

Before you confirm, nothing is booked, so you can change or cancel anytime. Just call or text (470) 279-6694. After you confirm, let us know as early as you can.
